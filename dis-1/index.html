The History of Halloween

Halloween, celebrated on October 31st, has roots in the ancient Celtic festival of Samhain. The Celts believed that on this night, the boundary between the living and the dead became blurred, allowing ghosts to roam the earth.

Over the centuries, Halloween evolved into a community-based holiday featuring costumes, trick-or-treating, and jack-o'-lanterns. Today it is one of the most widely celebrated holidays in the world.
